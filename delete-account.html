Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Your Sale Buddy data can be in up to three places: on your device, in a hidden backup file in your own Google Drive, and, if you use Shop and Staff, in your shop account in the cloud. Use the steps below to delete any of them.

Delete your Shop and Staff account

  1. Open Sale Buddy, then Settings, then Shop and Staff.
  2. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Enter your password, or choose your Google account again, then tap Delete Account.

This permanently deletes your shop account (your name, email address, and sign-in) and every shop you own, with its products, cost prices, customers, bills, stock changes, quotations, purchases, suppliers, expenses, cash counts, activity log, invite codes, and staff list. Your staff can no longer use those shops. If you joined someone else's shop, you are taken out of it and the shop stays for its owner. Nothing is kept afterwards. The data on your device stays until you delete it as described below.

Deleting the account does not cancel a Shop and Sync plan. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

No longer have the app? Email lloydzedds@gmail.com from the email address of your shop account, with the subject "Delete my Sale Buddy account". We delete the account and the data above within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Delete your Google Drive backup

  1. Open Sale Buddy and sign in with the Google account you want to clean up.
  2. Open Accounts and Backup, then Manage My Cloud Data.
  3. Tap Delete Cloud Backup.
  4. Type your Google account email to confirm, then tap Delete.

This permanently removes the Sale Buddy backup file for that account from Google Drive's hidden app data folder.

Delete the data on your device

  1. On your Android phone, open Settings, then Apps, then Sale Buddy.
  2. Open Storage and tap Clear data, or uninstall the app.

This removes the products, sales, customers, and settings stored on the device, for every account that has been used on it. Backups or exports you saved yourself, for example in your Downloads folder, are separate files that you can delete with your file manager.

Remove Sale Buddy's access to your Google account

Go to your Google Account's third-party access page, choose Sale Buddy, and select Delete all connections or Remove access. Delete the cloud backup first, because after access is removed the app can no longer delete it for you.

Need help?

If you cannot use the app to delete your data, contact lloydzedds@gmail.com and we will help you with the steps above. See also the Privacy Policy.
