Using Sale Buddy

Sale Buddy helps users record products, inventory, customers, sales, bills, returns, store details, reports, and backups. By using the app, you agree to use it lawfully and to review important business records for accuracy.

Account Options

You can use Sale Buddy locally without an account, or you can sign in with Google, or with an email address and password, to keep account data separate, use optional Google Drive app data backup features, and create or join a shop with other people.

Shops, Staff, And Roles

A person who creates a shop is its owner. The owner decides who joins by sharing a shop code, is responsible for everyone they invite, and can remove a person from the shop at any time. A code works once and runs out after 24 hours, so share it only with the person it is meant for. People who join as cashiers can sell and add stock but cannot see cost prices or profit. The app notes who made sales and changes in an activity log that the owner can read. You are responsible for telling your staff that this log exists and for following the laws that apply to your workplace.

We may stop a shop or an account from using shop features if they are used to harm others or to break these terms.

Prices PIN And Other Locks

The Prices PIN, App Lock, and cashier restrictions are conveniences to keep business information away from casual viewing. They are not a guarantee against someone who has your unlocked device, your PIN, or your account. Keep your PINs and passwords private. If you forget the Prices PIN, you can set a new one after confirming with your phone's own screen lock.

Quotations, Bills, And Tax

Quotations are price offers, not tax invoices. Bills, GST amounts, HSN codes, and reports are calculated from the prices, tax rates, and codes you enter. You are responsible for choosing the right rates and codes and for the returns and records you file with tax authorities.

Archiving Months

Sale Buddy asks you to close each month and moves that month's bills to Archived Bills only after you finish the steps. Archiving does not delete anything. You are responsible for checking the month, adding missing expenses, and saving the exports you need.

Loyalty Points

Loyalty points are a feature for shops to reward customers under rules the shop chooses. Points have no cash value, and the shop is responsible for honoring them and for any promise it makes to its customers.

Advertising And Purchases

Languages

Sale Buddy can be shown in several languages. Translations are provided for convenience and may contain mistakes or be missing for some screens. Bills and exports are printed in English. If a translation and the English text differ, the English text is the one that counts.
