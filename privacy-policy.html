Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Overview

Sale Buddy is a sales, inventory, billing, customer, return, and backup application for small shops. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app stores and how the optional Sale Buddy account and Google account features work.

Data You Enter

Sale Buddy stores the information you enter into the app, including products, prices, stock, barcodes, product images, customers, sales, returns, payment details, bill details, suppliers and their GST and contact details, purchases, expenses, your opening cash balance, store details, and app settings.

Local Storage

Your Sale Buddy data is stored on your device. If you continue without signing in, it stays only on your device. Local backup files are created only when you choose backup or export actions inside the app.

Your Sale Buddy Account (Optional)

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Google, with an email address and password, or with an Indian mobile phone number. These are ways into one Sale Buddy account: the same email address with Google and with a password is the same account, and an account started with a phone number is asked to add an email address, by Google or with a password, so it can also be opened with that email address. Your account decides which data opens on your phone, which Google Drive backups belong to it, and your shops in Shop and Staff.

Your account is kept by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your name, your email address, your phone number if you sign in with one or add one, the ways you sign in, and, if you set a password, that password in a protected form that Sale Buddy never sees. Firebase may send you an email to confirm your email address or, when you ask, to reset your password.

When you sign in with a phone number or add one, Google sends a one-time code to that number by SMS, and your mobile operator's standard rates may apply. To stop misuse, Google checks the request with Google Play Integrity or reCAPTCHA, and Google may store the phone number to prevent spam and abuse across its services, under Google's privacy policy. Your phone number is not shared with the other people in your shop.

Google Account And Google Drive App Data

If you sign in with Google, Sale Buddy uses your Google account to sign you in and to provide optional Google Drive app data backups. If you signed in with an email address or phone number, you can connect a Google account for backups; it is added to your Sale Buddy account.

Sale Buddy requests the Google Drive app data scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This allows the app to create, read, update, download, and delete its own hidden backup file in Google Drive's app data folder. Sale Buddy does not request access to view, list, edit, or delete your regular Google Drive files.

How Google User Data Is Used

Google user data is used only to sign you in, to keep each account's data separate, and for the cloud backup features that you choose to use. Sale Buddy uses Google Drive app data access to save, restore, download, and delete the app's own backup file.

Shop And Staff (Optional)

Shop and Staff lets a shop owner share products, customers, bills, stock and other shop records with the phones of the people who work in the shop. It is used only if you create or join a shop, and it uses your Sale Buddy account described above; it has no sign-in of its own.

When you create or join a shop, Sale Buddy stores the following in Google Cloud Firestore, a database that Google runs for Sale Buddy:

  • The shop's name, who owns it, and until when its Shop and Sync plan counts as paid.
  • For each person in the shop: their name, email address, role (such as owner, Cashier or Manager), what that role allows them to do, and when they joined. The roles the owner makes, with their permissions, are stored too.
  • Invitations the owner makes: a code that stops working after 24 hours, or an invitation to a person's email address, which stores that address and stops working after 7 days.
  • Product details: name, selling, wholesale and MRP prices, barcode, category, unit, GST rate, HSN code, low-stock level, expiry date, and storage location. Cost prices are stored too, and only the owner and staff the owner allows to see them can read them.
  • Product photos: a reduced copy of each product's photo, so every phone in the shop shows it.
  • Customers: name, phone number, price type, credit limit, GSTIN, and follow-up date.
  • Bills: bill number, date, items, quantities, prices, discounts, GST, the customer's name and phone number, how and when it was paid, any amount still owed, returns, payments collected later, and the name of the person who sold. Cost prices and profit are not stored with bills.
  • Stock changes: for each product, how many were sold, bought or adjusted, so every phone can work out the stock. For the owner and staff the owner allows to adjust stock, stock changed by hand is stored with the reason (such as damaged, expired or found), the quantity and its cost.
  • Table Orders, for shops that use them: the tables, the orders running on them, and the dishes, quantities and prices on each order, so waiters' phones and the counter see the same orders.
  • Quotations: the customer's name and phone number, the items, and the notes written on them.
  • Activity log: a short note of what was done on each phone, such as “Sale made”, with the staff name.
  • For the owner, and staff the owner allows to see purchases or expenses: suppliers (name, how they are registered for GST, GSTIN, state, phone number, email address, postal address, note), purchases with their items, prices, GST, state of supply and payments, expenses, and the daily cash counts. Other staff cannot read these.

Only the people in a shop can read its data, and staff can read cost prices, purchases, expenses and cash counts only if the role the owner gave them allows it. Loyalty points and photos of suppliers' bills stay on each phone and are not stored in the cloud. When a staff member signs out, leaves the shop or is removed, the shop's data is deleted from their phone; the shop keeps it. The data is sent encrypted. The developer can reach this database only to keep the service working, to fix problems, or when the law requires it, and never sells it or uses it for advertising.

The Shop and Sync plan is a Google Play subscription. Google Play handles the payment; Sale Buddy asks Google Play whether the plan is active and stores only until when the shop counts as paid.

Camera And Photos

Sale Buddy asks for camera permission only when you scan a barcode. Product photos and photos of suppliers' bills that you choose or take are kept on your device and are included in the backups you create. In a shop that uses Shop and Staff, product photos are also shared with the shop's other phones, as described above. Photos of suppliers' bills are never sent to the cloud by Sale Buddy.

App Lock, Notifications, Printing And Sharing

App Lock stores a scrambled (hashed) copy of your PIN on your device only. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; Sale Buddy never receives your fingerprint or face data.

The optional daily alert is created on your device from your own data, and no information is sent anywhere to make it. To print receipts, Sale Buddy connects over Bluetooth to a printer you have already paired and chosen. Sale Buddy does not use Bluetooth to find or track other devices or your location.

When you choose to send a bill or a payment reminder by WhatsApp, SMS, or another app, Sale Buddy only opens that app with the message ready. Nothing is sent until you send it, and the message then goes through that app under its own privacy policy.

Barcode Lookup

If you use the barcode lookup feature, the barcode number is sent to the public Open Food Facts and Open Products Facts databases to find a product name. No account details, store details, customer details, or sales are sent. Like any internet request, the service can see your IP address.

Device Date And Time Check

Sale Buddy checks that your device clock is correct by requesting a small page from Google. No app data is sent. Like any internet request, Google can see your IP address. If you are offline, the app keeps working.

Advertising And Remove Ads Purchase

Sale Buddy is free and shows a small banner ad on some screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Depending on where you live, Sale Buddy asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings under Ad Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses data in ads.

Ads are never shown on the sales, payment, or billing screens, and no products, sales, customers, or other data you enter are given to advertisers.

You can pay once to remove ads. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, which keeps the record against your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. Sale Buddy does not see your payment details.

To confirm that a purchase is real, the app sends Google Play's purchase token for it to Sale Buddy's server, which asks Google Play about it. For the Shop and Sync plan, the server keeps a scrambled (hashed) copy of the token, the Sale Buddy account that used it, the plan, until when it is paid, and the shops it pays for, so renewals, cancellations and refunds that Google Play reports can be applied. A new plan purchase carries a scrambled (hashed) form of your Sale Buddy account ID, so the plan can only pay for your own shops.

Crash Reports

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, Sale Buddy sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so the developer can fix the problem. A report holds the error and where in the app it happened, the app version, your phone model and Android version, and a random installation ID. It does not include your products, sales, customers, store details, or account email. Crash reports are not sent while you use a debug build. See Firebase privacy and security.

What We Do Not Collect

Apart from your account and the Shop and Staff data described above, which are stored only if you sign in or use that feature, the developer does not receive, store, or have access to your products, sales, customers, or backups. The only other data collection is the advertising and crash reports described above. This version of the app has no analytics. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Sharing

Sale Buddy does not sell your app data. Data is shared only when you choose actions such as sharing a bill, exporting a report, sharing a backup, saving a file to device storage, using Google Drive app data backup, or sharing your shop's data with the people in your shop through Shop and Staff.

Data Deletion

You can delete local data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. You can delete the Google Drive app data backup from Sale Buddy by opening Accounts and Backup, then Manage My Cloud Data, then Delete Cloud Backup. You can also manage connected app access from your Google Account.

To delete your Sale Buddy account, open Settings, then Shop and Staff, and tap Delete Account. This deletes your account with its name, email address, phone number and ways to sign in, every shop you own with its products, product photos, cost prices, customers, bills, stock changes, quotations, purchases, suppliers, expenses, cash counts, table orders, activity log, roles, invitations and staff list, and takes you out of shops you joined. You can also ask for this by email at the address below; it is done within 30 days.

Your data stays until you delete it. A Google Drive backup stays in your Drive until you delete it. For step-by-step instructions, see How to delete your data.

Security

Sale Buddy uses Google Sign-In, Google APIs, and Google Firebase for account, backup, and Shop and Staff features, and data sent to them is encrypted in transit. You are responsible for keeping your device, your password, your phone number's SIM and your Google account secure.

To keep modified copies of the app, emulators and scripts away from shop data and purchases, Sale Buddy uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity. When the app talks to Firebase or Sale Buddy's server, Google checks that it is the real app from Google Play on a genuine Android device and gives it a short-lived token. Google handles these checks under its own privacy policy; Sale Buddy receives only whether the check passed.

Children

Sale Buddy is intended for business and shop management use. It is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Sale Buddy changes. If important changes are made, the app may ask users to accept the updated terms before continuing.
