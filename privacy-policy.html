Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Overview

Sale Buddy is a sales, inventory, billing, customer, return, and backup application for small shops. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app stores and how optional Google account features work.

Data You Enter

Sale Buddy stores the information you enter into the app, including products, prices, stock, barcodes, product images, customers, sales, returns, payment details, bill details, store details, and app settings.

Local Storage

If you continue without a Google account, your Sale Buddy data is stored locally on your device. Local backup files are created only when you choose backup or export actions inside the app.

Google Account And Google Drive App Data

If you sign in with Google, Sale Buddy uses your Google account to keep data separate for that account and to provide optional Google Drive app data backups.

Sale Buddy requests the Google Drive app data scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This allows the app to create, read, update, download, and delete its own hidden backup file in Google Drive's app data folder. Sale Buddy does not request access to view, list, edit, or delete your regular Google Drive files.

How Google User Data Is Used

Google user data is used only to support account-based separation and the cloud backup features that you choose to use. Sale Buddy uses Google Drive app data access to save, restore, download, and delete the app's own backup file.

Shop And Staff (Optional)

Shop and Staff lets a shop owner share products, customers, bills and stock with their cashiers' phones. It is used only if you sign in to it. You sign in with Google or with an email address and password. Your account is kept by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your email address, your name, and, for an email account, your password in a protected form that Sale Buddy never sees.

When you create or join a shop, Sale Buddy stores the following in Google Cloud Firestore, a database that Google runs for Sale Buddy:

  • The shop's name, who owns it, and until when its Shop and Sync plan counts as paid.
  • For each person in the shop: their name, email address, role (owner or cashier), and when they joined.
  • Invite codes the owner makes, which stop working after 24 hours.
  • Product details: name, selling, wholesale and MRP prices, barcode, category, unit, GST rate, HSN code, low-stock level, expiry date, and storage location. Cost prices are stored too, and only the owner can read them.
  • Customers: name, phone number, price type, credit limit, GSTIN, and follow-up date.
  • Bills: bill number, date, items, quantities, prices, discounts, GST, the customer's name and phone number, how and when it was paid, any amount still owed, returns, payments collected later, and the name of the person who sold. Cost prices and profit are not stored with bills.
  • Stock changes: for each product, how many were sold, bought or adjusted, so every phone can work out the stock.
  • Quotations: the customer's name and phone number, the items, and the notes written on them.
  • Activity log: a short note of what was done on each phone, such as “Sale made”, with the staff name.
  • For the owner's phones only: suppliers (name, phone number, note), purchases with their items, prices and payments, expenses, and the daily cash counts. Cashiers cannot read these.

Only the people in a shop can read its data, and cashiers cannot read cost prices, purchases, expenses, or cash counts. Product photos and loyalty points stay on each phone and are not stored in the cloud. The data is sent encrypted. The developer can reach this database only to keep the service working, to fix problems, or when the law requires it, and never sells it or uses it for advertising.

The Shop and Sync plan is a Google Play subscription. Google Play handles the payment; Sale Buddy asks Google Play whether the plan is active and stores only until when the shop counts as paid.

Camera And Photos

Sale Buddy asks for camera permission only when you scan a barcode. Product photos you choose or take stay on your device and are included only in the backups you create.

App Lock, Notifications, Printing And Sharing

App Lock stores a scrambled (hashed) copy of your PIN on your device only. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; Sale Buddy never receives your fingerprint or face data.

The optional daily alert is created on your device from your own data, and no information is sent anywhere to make it. To print receipts, Sale Buddy connects over Bluetooth to a printer you have already paired and chosen. Sale Buddy does not use Bluetooth to find or track other devices or your location.

When you choose to send a bill or a payment reminder by WhatsApp, SMS, or another app, Sale Buddy only opens that app with the message ready. Nothing is sent until you send it, and the message then goes through that app under its own privacy policy.

Barcode Lookup

If you use the barcode lookup feature, the barcode number is sent to the public Open Food Facts and Open Products Facts databases to find a product name. No account details, store details, customer details, or sales are sent. Like any internet request, the service can see your IP address.

Device Date And Time Check

Sale Buddy checks that your device clock is correct by requesting a small page from Google. No app data is sent. Like any internet request, Google can see your IP address. If you are offline, the app keeps working.

Advertising And Remove Ads Purchase

Sale Buddy is free and shows a small banner ad on some screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Depending on where you live, Sale Buddy asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings under Ad Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses data in ads.

Ads are never shown on the sales, payment, or billing screens, and no products, sales, customers, or other data you enter are given to advertisers.

You can pay once to remove ads. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, which keeps the record against your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. Sale Buddy does not see your payment details.

Crash Reports

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, Sale Buddy sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so the developer can fix the problem. A report holds the error and where in the app it happened, the app version, your phone model and Android version, and a random installation ID. It does not include your products, sales, customers, store details, or account email. Crash reports are not sent while you use a debug build. See Firebase privacy and security.

What We Do Not Collect

Apart from the Shop and Staff data described above, which is stored only if you use that feature, the developer does not receive, store, or have access to your products, sales, customers, or backups. The only other data collection is the advertising and crash reports described above. This version of the app has no analytics. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Sharing

Sale Buddy does not sell your app data. Data is shared only when you choose actions such as sharing a bill, exporting a report, sharing a backup, saving a file to device storage, using Google Drive app data backup, or sharing products, customers, bills and stock with the people in your shop through Shop and Staff.

Data Deletion

You can delete local data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. You can delete the Google Drive app data backup from Sale Buddy by opening Accounts and Backup, then Manage My Cloud Data, then Delete Cloud Backup. You can also manage connected app access from your Google Account.

To delete your Shop and Staff account, open Settings, then Shop and Staff, and tap Delete Account. This deletes your account, every shop you own with its products, cost prices, customers, bills, stock changes, quotations, purchases, suppliers, expenses, cash counts, activity log, invite codes and staff list, and takes you out of shops you joined. You can also ask for this by email at the address below; it is done within 30 days.

Your data stays until you delete it. A Google Drive backup stays in your Drive until you delete it. For step-by-step instructions, see How to delete your data.

Security

Sale Buddy uses Google Sign-In, Google APIs, and Google Firebase for account, backup, and Shop and Staff features, and data sent to them is encrypted in transit. You are responsible for keeping your device and Google account secure.

Children

Sale Buddy is intended for business and shop management use. It is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Sale Buddy changes. If important changes are made, the app may ask users to accept the updated terms before continuing.
